# Cassotis Lexicon Attribution

The dictionary databases distributed with Cassotis IME are generated by
[Cassotis Lexicon](https://github.com/shenmin/cassotis-lexicon). They contain
transformed entries or ranking signals from the sources below. Raw source
archives are not redistributed in the IME package.

- Name: CC-CEDICT Chinese-English Dictionary
- Homepage: https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict
- Download: https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/export/cedict/cedict_1_0_ts_utf-8_mdbg.txt.gz
- License: CC BY-SA 4.0
- Copyright holder: MDBG and CC-CEDICT contributors

## THUOCL

- Name: THUOCL (Tsinghua Open Chinese Lexicon)
- Homepage: https://github.com/thunlp/THUOCL
- Download: https://github.com/thunlp/THUOCL/archive/refs/heads/master.zip
- License: THUOCL custom open terms (see the upstream README)
- Copyright holder: THUNLP and THUOCL contributors

THUOCL provides broad word-list and document-frequency signals. Filtered IT,
idiom/allusion, and medical subsets are used only as isolated vertical-layer

## Cassotis Project-Maintained Supplements

- Name: Cassotis curated lexical and vertical-layer supplements
- Homepage: https://github.com/shenmin/cassotis-lexicon
- License: CC BY-SA 4.0
- Copyright holder: Cassotis Lexicon contributors

Project-authored supplements cover daily/chat phrases, low-frequency exact
terms, pronunciation overrides, fiction and proper nouns, people and place
names, idioms/allusions, computing, civic, architecture, gaming,
game-development, and medical terminology. They remain separated into the
ranking layers documented by Cassotis Lexicon.

For the exact source versions and transformation rules of a particular
dictionary release, consult the corresponding Cassotis Lexicon release.
